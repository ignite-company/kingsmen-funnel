Before we begin

Your questions, answered.

What types of projects can I inquire about?

You can inquire about full-home renovations, kitchens, bathrooms, basement finishing, home additions and custom builds. Project feasibility is confirmed during your consultation.

How do I get an estimate?

Start by sharing a few details about your project. Scope, location, materials and budget are considered before an estimate can be prepared.

What areas do you serve?

This concept is designed for Ontario homeowners. Call us to confirm availability in your specific city or neighbourhood.

Do I need to have a design ready?

No. You can start with inspiration, a general idea or existing plans. The first conversation helps identify the appropriate next steps.
